06

Clear boundaries

You keep your words and your ownership.

We provide book design and print preparation. We do not take royalties or ownership of your manuscript.

Developmental editing, major rewriting, legal review, fact-checking, ISBN purchase, copyright registration, printing, and shipping require separate services or client action.

The approved manuscript must be locked before full layout begins. New content after that point can change the price and delivery date.

07

Common questions

Know what happens before you begin.

Can you work from a PDF?

Yes. A raw PDF can require reconstruction before editing and layout. PDF reconstruction starts at $150 after the file review.

Does the service include editing?

The core package includes light mechanical cleanup. Proofreading, developmental editing, and major rewriting require the premium package or a separate quote.

Can you upload the book for me?

The premium package includes upload support for one platform. You keep control of the publishing account, payment details, ISBN, and final submission approval.

When does production start?

Production starts after the deposit, complete intake, supplied files, and manuscript lock. The final balance is due after proof approval and before production files are released.
