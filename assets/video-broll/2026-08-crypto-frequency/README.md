# Before the Bull Run

## Recommended video title

**Before the Bull Run: Crypto, AI, and Building Your Watchlist**

### Alternate title options

- **Get Ready Before the Run: A Beginner’s Guide to Crypto Investing**

## Visual direction

InnerG editorial documentary stills: Philadelphia at night, brick red signal light, ink-black shadows, aged cream highlights, tactile grain, and enough negative space for captions. The images are intentionally text-free so they can support the narration without competing with it.

## Editorial note

Keep the financial language educational and grounded. The audio makes clear that this is not financial advice, so avoid overlays that imply a guaranteed return, a buy signal, or a specific asset recommendation.
