August Frequency: Financial and asset alignment.

This month is about getting money, focus, and assets into order. Clean up the loose ends, protect the budget, build the first layer of ownership, and move with a clear 30-day gameplan.

Use this prompt:

I want to create my August Gameplan. Help me turn my current life goals into a clear 30-day action plan I can actually follow.

Ask me to list what I am working on in money and admin, my main project or business goal, health and body, mental clarity and personal discipline, relationships or community, and one thing I keep avoiding.

Then organize my answers into a main objective, top five priorities, specific actions, what I should do in the next 12 hours, what I should track every morning and night, what I should review every Sunday, and what distractions or habits I need to remove.

Keep it direct, realistic, and action-based. No fluff. End with one question:

What is the one task I need to complete before my next check-in?

Monthly rhythm:

- Beginning of each month: new InnerGReads frequency and action prompt.
- Every Monday: AI news and practical tool notes from InnerG Intel.
- Every Sunday: review what moved, what stalled, and what needs a cleaner system.
