why i write.

my name is nasirr g. mayo. i’m a founder, builder, educator + author.

i write because i pay attention to the patterns around us. people. ownership. discipline. technology. relationships. culture. the choices we make + the futures those choices create.

writing gives me a place to slow those thoughts down, question them and leave something useful for people willing to spend time with the message.

i want innerg reads to stay open. u should be able to finish a piece before money enters the conversation.
