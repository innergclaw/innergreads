when i say actions, i am talking about the performance many people feel they have to become to receive acceptance, approval, attention, love, or access.

sometimes we repeat certain actions because those actions worked before. they got us noticed. they got us accepted. eventually those actions can become an identity.

when the action becomes the identity, what happens when that action no longer works?
